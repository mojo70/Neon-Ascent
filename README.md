# 🌃 Neon Ascent

**Neon Ascent** is a high-fidelity Cyberpunk Life RPG interface designed for the next generation of runners. Built with a focus on immersive UI and on-device intelligence, it serves as your personal neural-link to the sprawl.

---

## ⚡ System Overview

In the world of *Neon Ascent*, your device isn't just a phone—it's your **Cyberdeck**. 

### 🧬 Character Synthesis
Forge your digital ghost. The character creation module allows for deep customization of your cyber-frame, setting the foundation for your ascent through the corporate strata or the neon-lit gutters.

### 🖥️ The Cyberdeck Terminal
Your central command hub. A custom-built, neon-drenched interface featuring:
- **ICE Breach Protocol**: Secure your connection and bypass security layers.
- **Neural Core OS**: Integrated AI systems that adapt to your environment.
- **Data-Vault & Wallet**: Manage your encrypted assets and digital currency on the fly.
- **Live Terminal Feed**: Real-time telemetry and system logs straight from the undercity's datastreams.

### 🧠 Edge Intelligence (Neural Core)
*Neon Ascent* leverages **Google Gemma LiteRT** to provide sophisticated on-device AI. 
- **Local Mode**: Complete privacy. Your AI assistant runs directly on your hardware's NPU.
- **Cloud Sync**: High-bandwidth neural processing for complex operations.
- **Offline/Dark Mode**: Minimalist survival mode for when you're off the grid.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Specs (The Rig)

This project is built using the latest in mobile tech-ware:

- **Frontend**: 100% **Jetpack Compose** with custom shader-like Canvas rendering for that authentic neon glow.
- **Architecture**: Clean Architecture with **MVI/MVVM** patterns.
- **Intelligence**: **LiteRT (TensorFlow Lite)** for local LLM execution (Gemma).
- **Injection**: **Hilt** for modular, decoupled dependency management.
- **Async**: **Kotlin Coroutines & Flow** for handling high-frequency data pulses.
