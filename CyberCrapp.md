# CyberCrapp (CC) – Exercise Protocol Specification

**Version**: 2.1  
**Last Updated**: July 25, 2026  
**Purpose**: Pure training system definition. This document describes *what* CyberCrapp is as a lifting protocol. App implementation details live in `CCProgram.md`.

---

## Origin & Philosophy

CyberCrapp is a modern evolution of Dante Trudel’s classic DoggCrapp (DC) training.

Original DC is a high-intensity, low-volume system built on rest-pause sets, progressive overload via logbook, and extreme stretching. It works well for intermediate and advanced trainees who recover adequately, but it has clear limitations in 2026:

- Rigid blast/cruise structure
- Extreme stretching that can be joint-taxing
- No deliberate lengthened-position emphasis
- Limited auto-regulation
- Frequency that can feel opaque to new users

**CyberCrapp keeps the soul of DC** (brutal efficiency, rest-pause intensity, logbook obsession) while upgrading the weak points with current evidence on frequency, lengthened partials, recovery management, and progressive overload.

---

## Core Tenets

1. **High Effort, Controlled Volume**  
   One primary compound per muscle group per session. Quality over quantity. Rest-pause clusters + lengthened partials deliver effective stimulus without excessive systemic fatigue.

2. **Progressive Overload is Non-Negotiable**  
   Weight is held constant while chasing total cluster reps. Once the top of the rep range is hit, weight increases on the next session. The logbook is the source of truth.

3. **Lengthened Position Emphasis**  
   After the rest-pause cluster, 3–5 controlled lengthened partials in the stretched position. Current research supports stretch-mediated hypertrophy advantages.

4. **Smart Recovery Management**  
   Loaded stretches are capped at 30–45 seconds. Rest intervals default to 15 seconds between mini-sets (user-adjustable). Deloads are planned and reactive.

5. **Frequency That Matches Evidence**  
   3-way rotating A/B/C split targeting approximately 2× per muscle group every 8–9 days.

6. **Personalization Over Dogma**  
   Experience level, injuries, and recovery signals modify application. No sacred cows.

7. **Powerbuilding Hybrid**  
   Controlled eccentric + explosive (safe) concentric on every exercise that allows it. One primary compound per rotation can be emphasized for pure power intent when desired.

8. **Honest Progression Rules**  
   Fail to beat the log twice on the same exercise → rotate to a similar substitute. Old exercises can rotate back in later if the new one stalls.

## Split Structure

**Default**: 3-way rotating A / B / C  
Each muscle group is trained roughly twice every 8–9 days.

**Sample Mapping** (fully customizable):

**A – Push**  
1. Flat Bench Press (or variation) – full CC cluster  
2. Military Press – full CC cluster  
3. Overhead Single-Arm DB Tricep Extension – accessory

**B – Pull**  
1. Weighted Pull-Ups or Weighted Chin-Ups – full CC cluster  
2. Bent-Over Barbell Rows – full CC cluster  
3. Jerry Curls (Jerry Evans style) – biceps cluster or accessory

**C – Legs / Posterior**  
1. Back Squat or Zercher Squat – heavy set + widowmaker  
2. Romanian Deadlift – full CC cluster or straight sets  
3. Optional accessory (calves, etc.)

---

## Session Structure (Primary Compounds)

1. **Warm-up**  
   1–2 progressive sets for most loads. Scale to 3 sets when the working weight is very heavy (example: 225 → 315 → 405 before a 500 lb squat).

2. **Rest-Pause Cluster**  
   - One working set broken into 3 mini-sets.  
   - Default rest between mini-sets: **15 seconds** (adjustable 10–25 s).  
   - Target 0–2 RIR on each mini-set.  
   - Log total cluster reps + RIR.

3. **Cyber Finisher – Lengthened Partials**  
   3–5 controlled partials in the stretched position immediately after the final mini-set.

4. **Loaded Stretch**  
   30–45 seconds (shorter end for endomorphs). Toned from original DC extreme stretches.

5. Move to next exercise or finish session.

Typical session length: 45–60 minutes.

**Rest between full exercises**: 2–3 minutes (up to 3–5 minutes for very heavy compounds).

## Rep Ranges & Progression Rules

**Primary Rule**  
Weight is held constant. Chase total cluster reps. Once the top of the range is reached, increase weight on the next session. After a weight jump, the first session only needs to hit the minimum of the range.

| Movement Type | Range | Notes |
|---------------|-------|-------|
| Most upper body compounds & isolation (chest, shoulders, triceps, back width, biceps) | 11–20 total cluster | Hit top → increase weight |
| Weighted pull-ups / chin-ups | 11–20 | Same |
| Rows / back thickness | 10–15 total cluster | Form priority |
| Deadlift / heavy rack pulls | 6–9 straight sets | No rest-pause |
| Squats / main quad movements | 6–10 heavy + 20 widowmaker | Classic structure kept |
| Hamstring isolation | 15–25 | Higher is fine |
| RDLs | 10–15 | Treat closer to thickness work |
| Calves | 10–15 controlled | Optional bottom stretch pause |

**Exercise Rotation Rule**  
If you fail to beat the previous log entry on the same exercise **two consecutive times**, rotate to a similar substitute. The old exercise remains in the library and can be rotated back in later if the new one stalls.

## Power Element

Every exercise that can safely be performed explosively uses:
- Controlled eccentric
- Maximum safe force / speed on the concentric

This is the default for all suitable compounds and isolations. Safety overrides when injury flags or movement risk is high.

---

## Deload / Cruise

**Soft Deload (default)**  
- Trigger: Every 4–6 weeks **or** recovery signals (stagnation, low recovery score, joint notes).  
- Duration: One full A/B/C rotation + 1–2 extra rest days (~7–10 days).  
- Changes:
  - Keep the same exercises.
  - Convert rest-pause clusters into 2–3 straight sets.
  - Target 3–4 RIR.
  - Drop or minimize lengthened partials.
  - Shorten or skip loaded stretches.
- Goal: Recover joints, CNS, and motivation while maintaining movement patterns.

Harder “cruise” style resets remain available for longer recovery needs but are not the default.

---

## Accessories

- Primary compounds always use the full CC structure (cluster + partials + stretch).
- Standard accessories follow the body-part rep ranges above, performed as 1–2 hard straight sets or light clusters.
- Specialization sub-protocols (high-volume arm work, etc.) may break normal rules when deliberately selected.

---

## Jerry Curls (Jerry Evans Style)

One-arm dumbbell curl performed standing with the working arm beside the body (plane of movement parallel to the torso).  

Bottom position: palm faces away from the thigh, back of the hand lightly rests against the side of the thigh.  

Curl with strong supination, drive a tight peak squeeze at the top (elbow moves slightly inward), and emphasize an intense controlled stretch at the bottom.  

Strict form. One arm at a time.

## Summary

CyberCrapp is a high-intensity powerbuilding protocol that preserves the efficiency and progressive spirit of classic DoggCrapp while incorporating lengthened partials, clearer progression rules, modern deload practices, and safety-conscious tempo guidelines.

This document defines the *training system*.  
See `CCProgram.md` for how the system is implemented inside the app.
