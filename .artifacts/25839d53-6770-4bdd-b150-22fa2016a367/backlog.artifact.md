# 📅 Future Training Missions

This backlog tracks upcoming features and refinements for the Neon Ascent training system.

## ⚙️ Equipment & Availability
- `[ ]` **Equipment Profiles**: Setting to define available hardware:
    - *Options*: Full Gym, Barbell Only, Dumbbell Only, Kettlebell Only, Hammer Strength, Cable Machines.
    - *Impact*: Automatically hides routines and exercises requiring unavailable equipment.
- `[ ]` **Exercise Blacklist**: Ability to mark specific exercises as "Unavailable at my location."
- `[ ]` **Global Reset**: "Restore All Exercises" setting to clear blacklists and reset availability.

## 🏃 Endurance Hybrid
- `[ ]` **Auxiliary Missions**: Track Zone 2 and HIIT runs around the lifting rotation.
- `[ ]` **Recovery Awareness**: Warnings when scheduling HIIT on days adjacent to high-intensity leg sessions.

## 🧠 Advanced Auto-Nudges
- `[ ]` **Volume Calibration**: AI suggestions to add/remove sets based on HRV and sleep trends (once wearable deep-sync is finalized).
